WHITE #1

Vienna

1.e4 e5 2.Nc3

Fast development, pressure on e5, and tactical chances without being an all-in gamble every game.

WHITE #2

King's Gambit

1.e4 e5 2.f4

Maximum chaos: trade a pawn for time, open lines and attacking chances.

BLACK #1

Caro-Kann

1.e4 c6 2.d4 d5

Reliable structure and good king safety while still giving you counterplay.

BLACK #2

French Winawer

1.e4 e6 2.d4 d5 3.Nc3 Bb4

The aggressive Black choice: pin the knight, attack the center and fight for ...c5 counterplay.

How to train

Watch a video → practice the opening line → quiz yourself → replay the line until you can explain why each move belongs there.

VIENNA

Core plan

1.e4 e5 2.Nc3. Develop quickly, pressure e5, and use f4 when it is justified by the position.

KING'S GAMBIT

Core plan

1.e4 e5 2.f4. Your pawn sacrifice buys time and open lines. Spend that time on development and initiative.

CARO-KANN

Core plan

1.e4 c6 2.d4 d5. Challenge the center and keep the c8 bishop from getting buried behind the pawn chain.

WINAWER

Core plan

1.e4 e6 2.d4 d5 3.Nc3 Bb4. Pin the knight, pressure White's center, and look for the right moment for ...c5.
