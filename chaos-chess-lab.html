♟ Chaos Chess Lab

Original training for our aggressive repertoire: Vienna + King's Gambit as White, Caro-Kann + French Winawer as Black.

WHITE #1

Vienna

1.e4 e5 2.Nc3

Fast development and tactical pressure; add f4 when the position supports it.

WHITE #2

King's Gambit

1.e4 e5 2.f4

Maximum chaos: trade a pawn for time, lines and initiative.

BLACK #1

Caro-Kann

1.e4 c6 2.d4 d5

Solid structure, active bishop development and good king safety.

BLACK #2

French Winawer

1.e4 e6 2.d4 d5 3.Nc3 Bb4

Pin the knight, hit the center and create an unbalanced fight.

Study rule

For every move ask: What does it attack? What does it stop? What pawn break comes next?

VIENNA

Core idea

1.e4 e5 2.Nc3. Develop, pressure e5, then use f4 when it gains something concrete.

KING'S GAMBIT

Core idea

1.e4 e5 2.f4. Open lines and make your development tempo count.

CARO-KANN

Core idea

1.e4 c6 2.d4 d5. Challenge the center while keeping the light bishop free.

WINAWER

Core idea

1.e4 e6 2.d4 d5 3.Nc3 Bb4. Pin the knight and prepare central counterplay, especially ...c5.
